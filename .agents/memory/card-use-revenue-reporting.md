---
name: Card-use revenue reporting
description: The business rule for the owner-facing monthly and daily totals when a customer uses a treatment card.
---

For the additional revenue totals that exclude card-paid shift earnings, count card purchases on their purchase dates and count walk-in service-shift earnings. Do not add the employee-pay amount from a shift linked to a treatment card, and do not estimate card redemption money from minutes or employee service rates.

**Why:** the user explicitly clarified that card purchases belong in card-sales totals, while a massage logged against a treatment card must not appear in or increase the two additional totals. The app does not record the customer's cash value for a card redemption.

**How to apply:** keep the existing overall report separate from these additional totals. Preserve the distinction in both daily and monthly reports, and test with card-paid and walk-in shifts on the same day.
