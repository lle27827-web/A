---
name: Appointment access
description: Access policy for facility appointments containing customer contact details
---

Facility appointment records are managed separately from employee, card, revenue, and service permissions. Owners can manage all appointments; managers need appointment-specific permission to view them, and a separate edit grant to create, change, or remove them. Editing implies viewing. Appointment records belong to the facility, not an individual employee notebook.

**Why:** the user selected a dedicated appointment permission for managers rather than inheriting access from another facility privilege.

**How to apply:** when changing appointment routes or navigation, enforce the dedicated view/edit grants on the server and keep facility appointments separate from personal notebook data.
