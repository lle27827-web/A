---
name: Appointment access
description: Access policy for facility appointments containing customer contact details
---

Facility appointment records are managed separately from employee, card, revenue, and service permissions. Owners can manage all appointments; managers need appointment-specific permission to view them, and a separate edit grant to create, change, or remove them. Editing implies viewing. Managers may delegate appointment access only if they already have the matching permission, and they cannot alter their own grant. Appointment records belong to the facility, not an individual employee notebook. Keep the appointment date separate from the free-text request: the customer may enter a preferred time and employee in the request field, while general notes remain separate. Recording a card-backed service session automatically completes scheduled appointments for the same customer on the same Ho Chi Minh business date when either normalized name or phone matches; this derived status change does not grant appointment-list access.

The appointment screen also shows current staff activity. Because service-session records do not contain a separate work-start timestamp, treat `createdAt` as the start and `minutes` as the expected duration; this is an estimate, not a clocked-out event. Show the current shift's estimated finish while working and the latest completed shift's estimated finish after the employee becomes available or off. Use “Đang rảnh” for the available status. For each employee, show the nearest future assigned appointment's date/time, customer name, request, and note in the activity status; omit past appointments from that status while retaining them in the appointment list. A separate current-day availability record stores manual “off” status without changing attendance/payroll rows; an active service session takes precedence while its estimated interval is still running. Keep non-working staff in the fair-rotation order based on recent work counts, place working staff after them, and order active staff by start time so the newest active shift is last. Refresh activity immediately after a service session is created, edited, or deleted; polling remains a fallback for changes from other clients.

**Why:** the user selected dedicated appointment permissions, asked card usage for the same customer/date to complete the appointment automatically, clarified that the request field should hold preferred time and employee separately from notes, wants newly working staff moved to the end of the activity queue, asked to see when the previous shift finished, and requested upcoming appointment details while hiding past appointments from status.

**How to apply:** enforce dedicated view/edit grants on appointment routes and navigation. Perform automatic completion server-side in the card-use transaction, comparing normalized name or phone and the local business date; do not expose appointment rows to managers lacking view permission. Keep facility appointments separate from personal notebook data. If actual shift start/end timestamps are added later, use those instead of the estimated interval from record creation.
