---
name: Theme profiles
description: Product requirements for keeping the built-in SỔ CÔNG themes independent from user-created color customization.
---

Keep the built-in yellow and dark-brown themes as separate defaults. Users may edit either preset only through its explicit edit action; opening the general color editor creates/edits a third, independent custom theme.

The personal timesheet keeps the yellow canvas in the yellow theme and uses brown for its card surfaces in both themes; its canvas is brown in dark mode. Action buttons across facility and personal attendance use red backgrounds with white text, and the SỔ CÔNG mark/name are red.

**Why:** The user wants custom color choices not to overwrite or unexpectedly alter the two built-in interfaces.

**How to apply:** When changing theme selection, palette storage, or theme controls, preserve independent palettes for the yellow, dark-brown, and custom profiles; keep reset actions scoped to the active profile.
