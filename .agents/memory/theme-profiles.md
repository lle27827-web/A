---
name: Theme profiles
description: Product requirements for keeping the built-in SỔ CÔNG themes independent from user-created color customization.
---

Keep the built-in yellow and dark-gray themes as separate defaults. Users may edit either preset only through its explicit edit action; opening the general color editor creates/edits a third, independent custom theme. Gray-filled surfaces across the app use the same light gray as the service add/delete buttons (#777777), including the revenue panels; the dark theme keeps its dark page background. Only the global search field and Settings gear keep brown fills.

Application text and numbers stay semibold or heavier, with headings and key figures receiving stronger emphasis for clear visual hierarchy. Use Be Vietnam Pro for Vietnamese interface copy and Manrope for display headings. Center both the monthly and daily revenue titles and totals inside their summary cards. The Settings gear remains brown; settings rows and edit controls use revenue gray with light text. Employee/card creation actions, service-selection controls, employee attendance/detail choices, and employee/card list surfaces use revenue gray with light text. Sign-out buttons are warm off-white with brown text. The personal timesheet canvas and header are yellow in both built-in themes; its card and row surfaces use revenue gray with bold white text. Home management shortcuts are white; home and return navigation controls use dark gray with white text across screens. The global search field is dark brown with light text. Editable fields remain white. Employee names and all text inside employee-detail and revenue panels must be pure white. Destructive actions retain red text and borders.

**Why:** The user wants custom color choices not to overwrite or unexpectedly alter the two built-in interfaces, prefers bold text, and most recently requested dark-gray home/return buttons across all screens while keeping the management shortcuts white. They also requested white text for card and employee names, so explicit scoped overrides are needed after page-level foreground rules. Keep button labels dark on white controls even when their containing panel forces white text, and avoid opacity that makes disabled labels unreadable. A hierarchy keeps bold text readable and makes headings and figures easier to scan.

**How to apply:** When changing theme selection, palette storage, or theme controls, preserve independent palettes for the yellow, dark-gray, and custom profiles; keep reset actions scoped to the active profile. Migrate legacy gray surface values in saved palettes to #777777 once, without overwriting subsequently customized colors or changing the search field and Settings gear. Keep app text semibold or heavier, emphasize headings and key figures, and use Be Vietnam Pro/Manrope as the interface/display font pair. Center the revenue title and amount in both the monthly and daily summary cards. Preserve the yellow personal canvas, dark page background in the dark theme, white editable fields and home shortcuts, dark-gray home/return navigation controls with white labels, white text in employee/card names and revenue panels, dark labels on white controls, fully legible disabled controls, and red destructive actions.
