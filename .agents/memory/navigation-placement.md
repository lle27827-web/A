---
name: Navigation placement
description: User-requested placement of back/home actions across SỔ CÔNG.
---

Place “Trở về trang chính” and “Trở về” after page content and lists, not above them. This applies to search results and employee-view mode as well as normal pages.

**Why:** the user requested these controls at the end of every list.

**How to apply:** preserve back-history and selected-month behavior when adding pages or changing layouts; do not reintroduce duplicate navigation at the top. Modal close/cancel controls are not affected.

Keep the service-and-price catalog link on the home screen, not in Settings. Preserve “Sửa dịch vụ và giá” as a manager permission in access grants.

**Why:** the user confirmed the catalog shortcut should be removed from Settings while the home-screen entry and manager permission remain available.

**How to apply:** do not remove or rename the grant permission when changing Settings navigation.
